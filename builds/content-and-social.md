# Content and social: community builds with Jev

9 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/content-and-social).

## [jevmeter: a live BS meter for any video](https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2100473581251748216)
By [Chetaslua](https://x.com/chetaslua), 2026-09-17

Scores every sentence of a video against a set of yes/no questions and renders the result as an on-screen meter.

How Jev is used: Each sentence goes to Jev with five yes/no questions. The author reports 1,191 calls for a full presidential debate at a total cost of about five cents.

## [Hide posts on X with plain-language rules](https://x.com/marcelpociot/status/2100520134481735729)
By [Marcel Pociot](https://x.com/marcelpociot), 2026-09-17

A browser extension that collapses posts on X matching rules written in everyday language.

How Jev is used: Each post in the feed is checked by Jev against the rules. The author reports it is fast enough to go unnoticed and very cheap to run.

## [Shapeshift: an input that becomes what you mean](https://github.com/anishfn/shapeshift)
By [Anish Gupta](https://github.com/anishfn), 2026-09-22

One text box that turns into an event card, checklist, timer, bill splitter and more as you type.

How Jev is used: One Jev call answers 14 questions about what you typed; dates, amounts and maths are handled by code. Works offline with a fallback classifier.

## [PromoSift: spot unlabelled promotions on X](https://github.com/web3w/promosift-extension)
By [Rich Wong](https://github.com/web3w), 2026-09-22

A Chrome and Edge extension that labels or folds promotional posts X doesn't mark as ads.

How Jev is used: Jev classifies visible posts into promotion categories with probabilities; you choose whether to label or hide them.

## [Slop Filter: hide AI-generated posts](https://github.com/adamnroman/slop-filter)
By [Adam Roman](https://github.com/adamnroman), 2026-09-20

A browser extension that hides AI-written posts and comments on X, LinkedIn, Reddit and YouTube.

How Jev is used: Jev scores each post for how likely it is to be AI-written; posts over your threshold fold away, and you can tune it with your own labels.

## [lkclean: a cleaner LinkedIn feed](https://github.com/stefw/lkclean)
By [stefw](https://github.com/stefw), 2026-09-19

A Chrome extension that collapses engagement bait, self-promotion and off-topic posts on LinkedIn.

How Jev is used: Every post is classified by Jev and the reason for each decision is shown on the post.

## [unsaidbrief: find the decisions a brief leaves out](https://x.com/Divyanshueth/status/2100997199102677033)
By [Divyanshu](https://x.com/Divyanshueth), 2026-09-18

Paste a project brief and see which decisions are missing or in conflict.

How Jev is used: Jev checks the brief against a list of decisions and flags gaps and conflicts, with the probabilities shown.

## [Jev Social: social media research agent](https://github.com/socai-io/jev-social)
By [socai](https://github.com/socai-io), 2026-09-18

Turns Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn into source-cited reports through your own browser.

How Jev is used: Jev chooses each next step (search, open a post, read comments or finish) and a local CLI carries it out in Chrome.

## [A tiny ad blocker, for fun](https://github.com/realZachi/typesafe-adblock)
By [mahmoud](https://github.com/realZachi), 2026-09-17

A Chrome extension that asks Jev whether each page element is an ad and removes it.

How Jev is used: The single judgement, 'is this element an ad?', is Jev's; everything else is plain code. The author is clear it's an experiment, not a real ad blocker.
