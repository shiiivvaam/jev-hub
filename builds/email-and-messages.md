# Email and messages: community builds with Jev

9 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/email-and-messages).

## [Jevmail: open-source Gmail triage](https://github.com/fazlerocks/jevmail)
By [Syed Fazle Rahman](https://github.com/fazlerocks), 2026-09-19

Sorts a Gmail inbox into Needs reply, Updates, Promos, Sales and Spam, read-only and running locally.

How Jev is used: Jev classifies each message and gives an urgency score; the author reports 1,000 emails in about a minute for around three cents.

## [A live sales copilot](https://x.com/moritzkremb/status/2102537239662096658)
By [Moritz Kremb](https://x.com/moritzkremb), 2026-09-22

A copilot that listens to a sales call and suggests what to say next.

How Jev is used: It tracks the call stage, follows the script, handles objections and shows live signals and a probability of closing.

## [jev-chat: a reply assistant that reads the chat first](https://github.com/jev-chat/jev-chat-jarvis)
By [jev-chat](https://github.com/jev-chat), 2026-09-21

Before you reply, it works out what the other person means and how risky the moment is, then drafts replies you can fill in with one tap.

How Jev is used: Jev judges intent and risk from the conversation on screen and helps pick the draft. Sending is always left to the person, and there are versions for Android, Windows, macOS and iOS.

## [JevChat for Windows: reply help beside the chat window](https://github.com/jev-chat/jev-chat-windows)
By [jev-chat](https://github.com/jev-chat), 2026-09-21

A Windows companion that reads the conversation on screen and offers three candidate replies.

How Jev is used: Local OCR reads the messages, Jev judges intent and mood, and one click fills a reply into the input box. Sending is always manual.

## [jev-chat for macOS: an on-screen reply assistant](https://github.com/jev-chat/jev-chat-jarvis-mac)
By [jev-chat](https://github.com/jev-chat), 2026-09-21

A read-only floating panel for macOS that explains what the latest message really means.

How Jev is used: It reads the chat window, then shows the likely intent, a risk level from 0 to 9 and suggested replies, using a local model and Jev. The author reports 86.4% on eight intent classes zero-shot.

## [Jev Chat Assistant: reply help for Windows chat apps](https://github.com/Liyucheng1997/332_lab-jev-chat)
By [Yucheng LI](https://github.com/Liyucheng1997), 2026-09-21

A non-intrusive layer that reads the conversation on screen and suggests replies.

How Jev is used: Jev judges the other person's real intent, a danger level and the best action, then a generative model drafts three replies that Jev ranks. Sending is always manual. The page is mostly in Chinese.

## [Crush Monitor: reading the mood in a chat](https://github.com/FerryCorleone/crush-monitor)
By [FerryCorleone](https://github.com/FerryCorleone), 2026-09-20

A small tool that analyses a conversation for emotion, intent and how well each reply landed.

How Jev is used: It shows the three most likely emotions and intents per line, a goodwill score and a grade for your replies. It supports Jev, OpenAI Decisions and text models, and runs on your own machine.

## [Monitoring 25 WhatsApp groups with Jev](https://x.com/t0t0_build/status/2101082444577567162)
By [t0t0](https://x.com/t0t0_build), 2026-09-18

A real-time monitor for client groups.

How Jev is used: Jev judges urgency, problems and unresolved orders, and tells a language model to message the owner when something needs attention. The author writes in Spanish.

A test of Jev on a real inbox.
