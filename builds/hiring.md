2 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/hiring).

## [Jev Recruiter: a LinkedIn recruiting agent](https://github.com/skeptrunedev/jev-recruiter)
By [skeptrune](https://github.com/skeptrunedev), 2026-09-18

A local recruiting workspace that browses LinkedIn profiles against a role.

How Jev is used: It is built on Browser Use's Jev Ultrafast; Jev chooses where to go, screens titles and assesses visible profile excerpts, and you keep the profiles and evidence.

## [Matching a candidate to 400 companies in 12 seconds](https://x.com/sarvagya_kul/status/2100980770206879849)
By [Sarvagya Kulshreshtha](https://x.com/sarvagya_kul), 2026-09-18

A job-matching demo that scores companies against one candidate profile.

How Jev is used: The author reports 12 seconds and $0.0005 to predict the best-fitting jobs, with a confidence score and mismatches flagged.
