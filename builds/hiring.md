# Hiring: community builds with Jev

4 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/hiring).

## [Rolewise: match a résumé to real job openings](https://x.com/zhilinjerrywag/status/2101576651238711642)
By [Zhilin Wang](https://x.com/zhilinjerrywag), 2026-09-20

Matches a résumé against live job descriptions.

How Jev is used: Another service retrieves the jobs; Jev makes the fit decision for each one.

## [Job Match: résumé review](https://github.com/hamidfarmani/jev-resume-match)
By [Hamid Farmani](https://github.com/hamidfarmani), 2026-09-20

Upload a résumé and a role to get an overall score, per-criterion scores and a suggested bullet order.

How Jev is used: Jev returns typed, explainable match scores rather than generated text.

## [jevsume: résumé scoring](https://x.com/unown1ne/status/2100817301348262301)
By [Imon Roy](https://x.com/unown1ne), 2026-09-18

Scores a résumé against a fixed framework or a custom job title in under a second.

How Jev is used: Jev reads the résumé and answers the scoring questions; the author reports a cost below $0.0002 per résumé.

## [cv-screen: screen a folder of CVs](https://github.com/gtaras7/typesafe-jev)
By [Giorgos Tarasidis](https://github.com/gtaras7), 2026-09-17

A local workbench that screens hundreds of CVs and shows a sortable shortlist.

How Jev is used: Jev answers a small fixed set of questions per CV; readable arithmetic turns them into a verdict, and the hiring policy can be edited without re-running Jev.
