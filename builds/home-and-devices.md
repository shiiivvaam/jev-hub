# Home and devices: community builds with Jev

2 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/home-and-devices).

## [Home Assistant TypeSafe integration](https://github.com/allenporter/home-assistant-typesafe)
By [Allen Porter](https://github.com/allenporter), 2026-09-20

A Home Assistant voice integration for fast intent routing and device control.

How Jev is used: Jev classifies what you said and picks the target device in under 200 ms, without generating text.

## [Jev for Home Assistant](https://github.com/AboveColin/HA-Jev)
By [Colin de Vries](https://github.com/AboveColin), 2026-09-17

Ask your house a question and get a number back you can automate on.

How Jev is used: Questions become sensors (a probability, a choice or a score), with actions for automations and a conversation agent.
