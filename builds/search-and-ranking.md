# Search and ranking: community builds with Jev

9 projects, each credited to its author and linked to the original. Jev Hub has not tested them: read the source before relying on one. Browse them all with filters on [jevhub.io/builds](https://jevhub.io/builds/topic/search-and-ranking).

## [Aayan's YC indexor: search 6,241 startups by description](https://github.com/Aayan-DEV/aayans-yc-indexor)
By [Aayan Asif](https://github.com/Aayan-DEV), 2026-09-22

A search app where matching Y Combinator company logos float up as you describe a startup.

How Jev is used: Companies are searchable by what they do, what their logo looks like and what the logo says, and results show how likely each match is. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac.

## [Searching 6,000+ YC startups in under a second](https://x.com/aaayandev/status/2102137061490794730)
By [Aayan](https://x.com/aaayandev), 2026-09-21

A search app over thousands of Y Combinator companies.

How Jev is used: Jev ranks startups by colour, niche, competitor, age or logo image. The author reports sub-second results and $2.70 of total testing costs.

## [webctl: a smart web search tool for agents](https://github.com/dorkitude/webctl)
By [Kyle Wild](https://github.com/dorkitude), 2026-09-20

A command-line search tool for agents that saves tokens.

How Jev is used: It runs several web searches and has Jev score each result against the original query and goal, so the agent reads fewer tokens.

## [jev-align: AI functions from your feedback](https://github.com/sutro-sh/jev-align)
By [Sutro](https://github.com/sutro-sh), 2026-09-19

An experimental CLI that builds calibrated AI functions from human labels.

How Jev is used: Finds the examples Jev is unsure about, asks you to label them, and improves the function's questions automatically.

## [Classifying Zillow listings by things you cannot filter](https://x.com/venturetwins/status/2101341075684434245)
By [Justine Moore](https://x.com/venturetwins), 2026-09-19

A demo of natural-language search over property listings.

How Jev is used: Jev classifies listings by architecture, renovation status or proximity to freeways. The author reports under 20 seconds and $0.18 for thousands of listings.

## [Intent-based search in Gmail](https://x.com/dabit3/status/2100960281769738433)
By [nader dabit](https://x.com/dabit3), 2026-09-18

A demo of searching an inbox by what you mean.

How Jev is used: Jev judges relevance to the intent, and the author suggests letting embeddings pull candidates first for a very large inbox.

## [keep.md: Jev for reranking and tagging](https://x.com/iannuttall/status/2100884132272181594)
By [Ian Nuttall](https://x.com/iannuttall), 2026-09-18

A test of Jev on Cloudflare Workers for a bookmarking product.
